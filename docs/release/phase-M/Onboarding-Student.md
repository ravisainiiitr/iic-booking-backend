# Student Onboarding

## Getting started

1. Sign in at https://equip.iitr.ac.in.  
2. Confirm you are linked to a faculty wallet / nomination as required by your department.  
3. Book only equipment you are authorized to use.  

## Workflow

1. Select equipment and available slot.  
2. Enter required experiment inputs carefully.  
3. After booking, coordinate sample delivery with your supervisor and lab.  
4. Mark **Sample Sent** when applicable.  
5. Download results when the lab marks them available.  
6. Use Remote Analysis only if the booking shows Analyze and your faculty/lab allows it.  

## Rules

- Follow lab safety and sample submission SOPs.  
- Do not share login credentials.  
- For payment/wallet issues, contact your faculty supervisor first.
