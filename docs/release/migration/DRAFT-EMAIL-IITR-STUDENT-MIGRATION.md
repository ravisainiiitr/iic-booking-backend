## Subject

New IIC Equipment Booking Portal — Booking opens Wed 30 Sep 2026, 9:00 PM | Information for IITR Students

## Body (plain text)

Dear Student / Research Scholar,

The Institute Instrumentation Centre (IIC), IIT Roorkee is launching the new IIC Equipment Booking Portal.

**Important schedule**

- Booking for the week commencing from **05 October 2026** will be accepted using the **new booking portal**.
- Booking will be opened as usual on **Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 9:00 PM**.

### What you should do now

1. Until the new booking window opens on **Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 9:00 PM**, continue creating new equipment bookings on the **existing IIC Booking Portal** (for earlier weeks only, as applicable).
2. Sign in to the **new portal** with **Channel-i** and complete your profile if prompted.
3. Request to **link your account to your Supervisor / Faculty wallet** in the new portal (required for most student bookings).
4. Ask your faculty supervisor to **approve** the wallet link request if it is pending.
5. From **Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 9:00 PM**, book slots for the week commencing **05 October 2026** (and onwards) only on the **new portal**.
6. You can still view previous bookings and account information on the old portal during the transition window.

### New portal

https://equip.iitr.ac.in

### Support

If you cannot link to a faculty wallet or need help with Channel-i login, contact IIC / your Supervisor.
