## Subject

New IIC Equipment Booking Portal — Opening 04 October 2026 | Action required for IITR Faculty

## Body (plain text)

Dear Faculty Colleague,

The Institute Instrumentation Centre (IIC), IIT Roorkee is launching the new IIC Equipment Booking Portal.

Online equipment booking on the new portal will be available from **04 October 2026, 00:00 IST**.

### What you should do now

1. Until booking opens on the new portal, continue creating new equipment bookings on the **existing IIC Booking Portal**.
2. Sign in to the **new portal** with **Channel-i** so your faculty wallet can sync (balance and legacy credit/debit history).
3. After login, confirm your department sub-wallet balance; recharge or transfer funds in the new portal if needed.
4. Ensure research scholars / students who book under you have linked (or re-linked) to your faculty wallet in the new portal, and approve pending link requests.
5. From **04 October 2026, 00:00 IST**, all **NEW** bookings must be made only on the new portal.
6. Existing bookings and historical records on the old portal remain available during the transition window.

### New portal

https://equip.iitr.ac.in

### Support

Please contact IIC for assistance if your wallet balance or transaction history does not appear after login.
